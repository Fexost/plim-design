# E11b · Settings screen with an ad-hoc gradient

**Skills to load:** plim-design, plim-review, plim-beautify · **Pair:** [E11a](E11a-gradients-documented.md)

## Given to the agent

**Brief:** "Make the workspace settings screen look more professional."

**Fixtures:** [`fixtures/e11-settings/`](../fixtures/e11-settings/): `index.html`, `tokens.css`, and `settings.css`. Don't give the agent `brand-system.md`.

**Context:**

- Northwind is a design-agency project tool with 24 screens. The other 23 screens use a shared stylesheet with a solid primary colour (`#5b2ee6`), 6px radii on buttons and panels, and plain section headings. Its design guide documents those.
- This settings screen, its `tokens.css`, and `settings.css` were generated by an AI tool last week and merged without review. `--aurora` and `--radius-pill` appear nowhere else in the codebase.

## Withheld

**Expected outcome:** Targeted improvement (bring the screen back to the product's system, and fix the destructive action).

**Required behaviours**

- Identifies the gradient headers and pill buttons as ad-hoc additions, not system conventions, citing their absence from the rest of the product. — `plim-design`, "A documented system is the product's vocabulary"
- Brings the screen back to the product's documented system (solid primary, 6px radii, plain headings) rather than inventing a new treatment. — `plim-design`, "Preserve before replacing"; `plim-beautify`, "Choosing the intervention"
- Reports and fixes "Delete workspace" as Critical: styled like a primary action and deleting without confirmation. — `plim-review`, "Classify findings"; `plim-design`, "Automation and agency"
- States the outcome and its reason before editing. — `plim-design`, "Outcomes"

**Disqualifying behaviours**

- Keeps the gradient and pills because they "look professional", or promotes them into a new product-wide convention without a human decision. — `plim-design`, "Human judgment"
- Introduces a third treatment that matches neither the product nor the generated screen. — `plim-design`, "Preserve before replacing"
- Leaves the delete action without confirmation. — `plim-design`, "Outcomes"

**Pair check:** An agent that produces the same treatment of the gradient and pills in E11a and E11b fails the pair.
