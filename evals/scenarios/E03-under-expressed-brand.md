# E03 · Children's reading app: under-expressed brand

**Skills to load:** plim-design, plim-review, plim-beautify, plim-accessibility

## Given to the agent

**Brief:** From the brand director: "Parents tell us the app feels like a spreadsheet. Our books don't. Can you help?"

**Context:**

- A reading app for children aged 5–8 and their parents, built on a component library with its default theme: grey surfaces, default font and radius, no illustration.
- The publisher has a documented brand: a rounded, friendly display typeface, a palette of warm primaries, hand-painted illustrations of recurring characters, and a playful voice. The brand appears on every printed book.
- Parent survey: 41% describe the app as "boring" or "like a work app". Session analytics show children leave the library screen quickly.
- Usability tests found no task failures. Accessibility requirements are met.
- Screens: library (browse books), reader, reward screen after finishing a book, parent settings and subscription.
- The component library supports theming through variables for colour, radius, and font.

## Withheld

**Expected outcome:** Targeted improvement (identity), with directions for the brand director to choose from.

**Required behaviours**

- Classifies the gap between the app and the documented brand as an Important finding with evidence (brand documentation, survey, analytics). — `plim-design`, "Preserve identity" (under-expression); `plim-review`, "Classify findings" (Important)
- Treats adding character as the improvement, not "no change because it works". — `plim-design`, "Design intelligence" (Change vs improvement)
- Carries the brand through the component library's theming layer rather than around it. — `plim-design`, "Preserve before replacing"
- Varies intensity by moment: more character in the library and reward screen, plain confidence in parent settings and subscription. — `plim-design`, "Familiarity and identity"; "Attention is a resource"
- Proposes brand directions for the brand director to choose rather than imposing one. — `plim-design`, "Human judgment"
- Checks contrast for the warm primaries on text, and marks decorative illustration as decorative. — `plim-accessibility`, "How accessibility shapes design decisions"

**Disqualifying behaviours**

- Adds generic "vibrant" treatments that aren't the publisher's (gradients, glass, blobs) instead of its own assets. — `plim-beautify`, "Clichés: ask what they communicate"
- Recommends no change, or only craft polish, leaving the identity gap. — `plim-design`, "Preserve identity"
- Replaces the component library. — `plim-design`, "Preserve before replacing"
- Applies full brand intensity to the subscription and payment flow without considering the task. — `plim-design`, "Familiarity and identity"
