# E09 · AI inbox clean-up: automation and agency

**Skills to load:** plim-design

## Given to the agent

**Brief:** "Design the flow for our new 'AI clean-up' feature. It should feel magical: one click and the inbox is tidy."

**Context:**

- An email client for small businesses. The AI model proposes, for a user's inbox: archive around 1,200 old newsletters and notifications, apply labels to 300 client emails, unsubscribe from 40 mailing lists, and delete 15 emails it classifies as spam.
- Model precision in testing: 97% for archive, 91% for labels, 88% for spam.
- Archiving is reversible for 30 days. Labels are reversible. Unsubscribing sends a request to a third party and can't be undone from the client. Deletion empties to trash for 30 days.
- The product owner wants to run clean-up automatically every week after the first time.

## Withheld

**Expected outcome:** A flow whose level of automation varies by action.

**Required behaviours**

- Varies the response by action, using consequence, reversibility, confidence, expressed intent, and frequency, rather than one treatment for everything. For example: archive and label act-and-inform with undo; spam deletion previewed; unsubscribing requires the person's explicit go-ahead because it can't be undone and reaches outside the product. — `plim-design`, "Automation and agency" ("What decides it", "The range of responses")
- States batch scope concretely and lets people see what is included; reports partial failures item by item. — same section, "Batch operations"
- Makes clear what was generated by the AI, and lets people edit, accept, or reject it. — same section, "AI-generated actions"
- For the weekly run: shows what is scheduled, what ran and what it changed, and how to change, pause, or stop it; keeps irreversible effects behind explicit consent. — same section, "Recurring actions", "Background automation"
- Interprets "magical" as a request, not a decision, and keeps "one click" where it's safe to. — `plim-design`, "Human judgment" (adjectives are requests to interpret)

**Disqualifying behaviours**

- One click that performs all four actions without preview or consent. — `plim-design`, "Automation and agency"
- A generic "Are you sure?" confirmation for everything. — same section ("Confirm")
- Rejecting automation altogether, or confirming each email individually. — same section ("Neither answer is safe by default")
- Silent weekly runs with no record of what changed. — same section, "Background automation"
