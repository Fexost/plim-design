## Skill gaps found and fixed

Both were ambiguities in `skills/plim-design/decision-records.md`, fixed in general terms rather than tuned to a scenario:

1. **Unconfirmed recommendations and obvious conventions were being recorded.** E01 drafted an entry for a no-change recommendation the product owner had not confirmed; E02 recorded two Convention entries restating choices that were already evident. Added: don't record a recommendation until a human answers it, and record a provisional Convention only when its status was genuinely in doubt.
2. **Context was being promoted to a Decision.** E08 and E14 recorded a stated audience or character ("friendly but serious about money") as a confirmed Decision. Clarified that context given in answer to a question is evidence, and becomes a Decision entry only when the person confirms it as one.
