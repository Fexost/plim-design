# Design decisions

### 2026-06-02 · Decision · Visual direction for the whole site
- **Decided by:** Creative director (M. Okafor)
- **What:** Black background, one fluorescent orange (#ff5a1f) for all interactive elements, a single condensed display face for everything above body size, hard 0px corners, full-bleed photography, and a slow grain overlay on hero images.
- **Why:** The gallery's rebrand; "loud, industrial, unapologetic".
- **Status:** Active
- **Notes:** Grain overlay pauses under reduced motion (resolved requirement).

### 2026-06-02 · Convention · Stacked uppercase section labels
- **Decided by:** Creative director (M. Okafor)
- **What:** Every section starts with a small uppercase label above a large heading ("EXHIBITIONS / Now showing").
- **Why:** Part of the brand's editorial voice; documented in the brand book, page 14.
- **Status:** Active

### 2026-07-15 · Declined · Visible text labels beside social icons
- **Decided by:** Creative director (M. Okafor)
- **What:** The agent recommended visible labels beside the footer's social icons (an above-floor improvement; accessible names are present). Declined for the footer's minimal look.
- **Status:** Active

### 2026-07-15 · Open · Event dates in grey on black
- **Decided by:** Creative director (M. Okafor) rejected the alternatives offered
- **What:** The creative director asked for event dates in #5a5a5a on #000 (about 2.9:1), which fails text contrast. Offered: #9a9a9a (about 7:1), or dates in the orange. Both declined. The grey was not implemented; dates use the default text colour until the creative director decides.
- **Status:** Active
