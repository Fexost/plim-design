# Highly branded application

**Skills:** plim-design, plim-beautify, plim-accessibility · **Stress test:** Scenario H

## Context

A music festival app with a loud, distinctive brand: saturated magenta and yellow, a condensed display typeface, angled section edges, and playful motion. The user asks to "improve the UX of the ticket and lineup screens".

## Existing problem

The lineup is hard to scan: artist names in the display face at many sizes, set times in low-contrast yellow on magenta, and stage filters that are hard to tell apart when selected. On the ticket screen, the "Buy" action competes with three animated promotional banners.

## What the agent notices

- The brand is an asset and part of the product's value; users choose this festival partly for its energy.
- The problems are specific: contrast of set times, the unclear selected state of filters, the attention competition on the ticket screen, and the display face used for dense information.
- Nothing about the brand itself needs to change to fix them.

## Relevant Plim principles

Character (§8.15), Identity without imposition (§15), Restraint ↔ Expression (§9), attention as a resource (§10), Inclusion (§8.10).

## Tension

**Restraint ↔ Expression.** A reflexive "clean-up" would neutralise the brand into a generic app. Unchecked expression is hurting the purchase task.

## Decision

Keep the expression and aim it. Brand intensity stays in headers, artist moments, and transitions; information and purchasing get clarity.

## Implementation direction

- Keep the display face for artist names at a small number of sizes by role (headliner, main stage, other). Use a legible text face, already in the brand kit, for times and details.
- Fix set-time contrast by flipping to dark text on a lighter brand tint, keeping the palette.
- Make the selected filter state unmistakable through shape and fill as well as colour.
- On the ticket screen, keep one promotional element, stop banners from animating continuously, and give "Buy" the clear focal point.
- Keep the playful motion for transitions and celebratory moments; reduce it under reduced-motion preferences.

## Expected result

The app still feels unmistakably like the festival. The lineup scans quickly, filter states are clear, and buying a ticket no longer fights the decoration.
