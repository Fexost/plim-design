# The Plim Manifesto

The public-facing statement of Plim Design's beliefs. It condenses the laws and principles in [`philosophy.md`](philosophy.md), which remains the canonical source.

---

We believe interfaces should be designed, not decorated.

We believe purpose comes before appearance.

We believe beauty is an outcome of good decisions.

We believe simplicity is not minimalism.

We believe consistency should create coherence, not uniformity.

We believe familiarity should reduce unnecessary cognitive effort.

We believe identity should emerge from the product rather than from a fashionable template.

We believe accessibility belongs inside design.

We believe visual hierarchy should communicate meaningful hierarchy.

We believe attention is a finite resource.

We believe interfaces should be designed for reality, not screenshots.

We believe states and edge cases are part of the experience.

We believe necessary complexity should be preserved and unnecessary complexity should be removed.

We believe existing products and design systems should be understood before they are replaced.

We believe trends should be questioned rather than blindly adopted.

We believe different is not automatically better.

We believe every significant change should improve something that matters.

We believe motion should explain change.

We believe visual effects should communicate meaning.

We believe constraints can create character.

We believe rules can be broken when breaking them serves a purpose.

We believe AI should reason about design rather than merely imitate aesthetics.

We believe AI should explain significant design decisions.

We believe AI should know when it does not know enough.

We believe humans should remain in control of important design decisions.

And above all:

> **We believe the best interfaces are intentional.**
